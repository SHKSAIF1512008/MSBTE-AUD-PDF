01
COLLECTED INFORMATION

Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information when you use MSBTE Audiobooks Library:

  • Personal Information: such as your name and email address, if voluntarily provided by you, for example when contacting us or submitting a copyright notice.
  • Usage Data: such as pages visited, content accessed, time spent on the website, browser type, device type, and general navigation behavior.

Information collected through MSBTE Audiobooks Library is used for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the website and its educational resources.

  • To protect the security and integrity of the website and prevent misuse.

WEBSITE TECHNOLOGIES

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

MSBTE Audiobooks Library may use cookies, local storage, and similar tracking technologies to enhance website functionality, remember user preferences, and analyze site traffic.

Cookies are small data files stored on your device. You may configure your browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being used; however, doing so may affect certain features or functionality of the website.

We may also use third-party analytics tools to help us understand how visitors use the website. These tools may collect information such as your IP address and browsing behavior in accordance with their own privacy policies.
